# Retail Technical Seo

Check status, final URL, canonical, robots rules and sitemap membership for representative homepage, collection and product URLs. Classify HTTP 429 and blocked fetches as unverified and retry only through a permitted normal browser path. A sitemap entry establishes discovery, not Google indexing.
Inspect Product or ProductGroup JSON-LD in rendered pages and compare price, currency, availability and variant identity against visible offers. Separate field Core Web Vitals from lab data. Capture exact URLs and discrepancies before drafting a change.
Example: an INR page whose offer markup says USD merits a concrete fix. A script-stripped fetch alone cannot demonstrate missing markup.

Save findings and proposed edits in a local draft. Include current and proposed values, evidence, and the checks to run after release. Live changes and publishing require the user’s approval after reviewing the draft.
